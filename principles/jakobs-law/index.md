- 简介 / Tagline: 用户大部分时间花在别的产品上,带着在那里养成的期望来到你的产品。利用既有心智模型;改版时别一次扔掉所有熟悉感。
- Tagline (EN): Users spend most of their time on other products and arrive with the expectations built there. Lean on familiar mental models; don't discard every familiar cue at redesign.

| name | type | default |
|---|---|---|

## 适用场景 / When to use

- 表单控件、radio、按钮等沿用实体对应物的心智模型(07§28) / Form controls, radios, buttons follow their physical-world mental models (07 §28)
- 改版提供「回到旧版」过渡期(YouTube 2017 案例) / Redesigns ship with a temporary old-version escape (YouTube 2017 case)
- 跨产品复用通行范式:搜索右上、设置齿轮、底部标签栏 / Reuse cross-product paradigms: search top-right, settings gear, bottom tab bar

## 何时不用 / When not to use

- 为差异而差异:把通用控件重新发明成「独一无二」的交互 / Differentiation for its own sake: reinventing common controls as 'unique' interactions
- 一次性移除所有熟悉线索且不留退路 / Removing every familiar cue at once with no escape hatch
