Bento Grid An array of unequal rounded slots on one CSS Grid, encoding information priority through cell size — the default shape of SaaS feature sections after Apple's 2022-09 keynote; it is a layout, not a color scheme. Community ★★★ Classic Brutalism Default, unstyled HTML as a style: system fonts, hard black/white contrast, 1–2px black borders, the browser default link blue, zero shadows and zero radius — a mindset of 'honest materials', not a visual recipe. Community ★★ Claymorphism Pastel bases, oversized radii, and a puffy 'inflated clay' look built from dual inner shadows plus an outer shadow — elements float above the page instead of being extruded from it, fixing neumorphism's contrast failure. Community ★★★ Flat Design The minimalist UI language: simple elements, typography and flat color — a discipline of no gradients, no highlights, no skeuomorphic shadows; the shared substrate of a whole generation of system languages (Metro→Material→iOS 7), measured by NN/g to slow interactions 22% on average when affordance cues are stripped. Community ★★ Frutiger Aero The 2005–2013 'nature + tech gloss' aesthetic: brisk sky-blue and grass-green fields, glass bubbles, elliptical specular highlights and watery glass texture — successor to Y2K and the forerunner of the glass era (Glassmorphism/Liquid Glass). Community ★★★ Cyberpunk Electric cyan / magenta / acid yellow on near-black, layered with scanlines, chromatic aberration and RGB channel split — 'glitch as atmosphere', with compressed monospace type and HUD readouts for a terminal voice. Community ★★★ Editorial Magazine Magazine-grade typographic discipline: the headline→standfirst→byline→body→caption hierarchy, generous whitespace ('editorial confidence'), 2/3/12-column grids with pull quotes, images as narrative partners — the classic serif-display × sans-body pairing. Community ★★★ Glassmorphism Frosted-glass panels: backdrop blur + translucent fill + a 1px hairline light border — only reads as glass over a rich, colorful backdrop. Community ★★★ Gel-Candy Design The sweet style of high-saturation candy colors + translucent gel material + capsule radii + elastic motion; its emotional axis is playful/warm, pairing with neo-brutalism's confrontational/brutal as the era's two maximalist expression routes. Community ★★ Holographic Chrome Two reflective material families — spectral iridescence and liquid-metal chrome: holographic gradients sweep across hues to mimic diffraction film, chrome lays down silver with a hard horizon reflection; specular sheen and micro diffraction stripes carry the texture, aimed at cinematic launches, fashion and music. Community ★★★ Maximalism More is more: ≥5 clashing hues, multi-family multi-orientation type, ≥3 layered patterns, collapsed margins — an 'ordered chaos' that fills every inch, the antithesis of minimalism. Community ★★★ Memphis Design The postmodern language of the 1981 Milan Memphis Group: zigzag/polka-dot/checkerboard geometric patterning, deliberately clashing turquoise×coral×yellow×black, and flat geometry with thick black outlines (circles/triangles/squiggles) that refuses to harmonize. Community ★★★ Minimalism Do more with less: two typefaces, a restrained palette (neutrals + one accent), a uniform spacing scale, whitespace instead of borders and cards — not a trend but the baseline. Community ★★ Skeuomorphism The UI tradition of mimicking real materials and mechanical controls: material textures (leather/brushed metal/paper/wood), a top-lit bevel lighting model, pressable and rotatable mechanical metaphors — reviving in 2026 as a four-branch 'new materials family'. Community ★★★ Neo-Brutalism Black outlines + zero-blur hard offset shadows + candy-colored geometric blocks: the commercialized branch of brutalism, wrapping conventional UX in loud shapes. Community ★★★ Neumorphism Elements share the page background color exactly; a dual shadow (dark on one side, light on the other) extrudes or presses them into the surface — a low-contrast soft-plastic look that peaked in 2020 and receded over accessibility failures. Community ★★★ Spatial UI visionOS's design language translated to the Web (a platform-paradigm borrowing): glass windows floating over an environment, depth-as-hierarchy (few large shadows), gaze-as-hover (big targets + clear focus rings), and the 'minimum immersion' motion discipline. Apple HIG ★★★ Soft-Glow Design A dark-stage design language that organizes hierarchy through light rather than color blocks: low-fill glass panels carry the nav layer, content stays on highly readable solid surfaces; every glowing element answers to a single directional light; glow color stays kin to the content's key color. Community ★★★ Swiss International Style Mathematical grids + neutral sans-serifs + one structural accent color: flush-left ragged-right, hierarchy built purely from size/weight/position, objective photography over illustration — today's 'web default language' is its direct legacy. Community ★★★ Y2K Retro-Futurism Late-90s/early-2000s techno-optimism: chrome and iridescent-oil-slick gradients, translucent jelly plastic, fluorescent accents over chrome silver, pixel type and low-res artifacts — nostalgia built from an imagined future. Community ★★★
