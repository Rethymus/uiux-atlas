Slot Spring (Segmented)

分段滑块弹簧 · segmented control spring · layoutId pill · 竹简槽

In a segmented control the indicator slides between fixed slots on a spring: one persistent bubble physically travels across N slots (interruptible, velocity-preserving) — the iOS segmented-control indicator feel brought to the web.

Apple HIG ★★★
Live demo official default
Parameters
Geometry▾
stiffness300

Spring stiffness k (mass = 1, ω₀ = √k). Default 300 from the '中·微弹 / medium-microbounce' tier in research 10 §2.3 (the recommended segmented-control default): ω₀≈17.3rad/s, t_peak≈262ms. Motion source default is 100 (research 10 §6.3). Higher = fiercer launch and faster arrival (overshoot ratio unchanged); lower = more lethargic.

damping25

Damping coefficient c. Default 25 pairs with stiffness 300 to give ζ=0.722, overshoot≈3.8% — the 'bouncy but quiet' band (research 10 §2.3). Higher approaches critical (ζ→1, overshoot gone); lower multiplies rebound, ζ<0.5 oscillates visibly. Counter-example: the community-circulated `500/50` is actually overdamped (ζ≈1.118) — compute ζ before copying (research 10 §2.3).

Motion▾
segments4

JS param (demo rebuilds slots on change). HIG's official qualitative rules: segments are typically equal-width, the control acts as a single selector, wide layouts take ≤5–7 segments and iPhone ≤5 (research 10 §2.4); the demo caps at 5 to honor the iPhone ceiling.

Code breakdown

Structure & DOM

The “bamboo-slip track” = a fixed rail with N equal-width slots + one persistent pill. The de-facto paradigm is Sam Selikoff’s AnimatedTabs: render exactly one bubble, slots are just its “homes”, switching = physical travel of that single element (research 10 §2.1, signature craft #2):

<div class="sl-rail" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Segmented control">
  <div class="sl-pill"><!-- persistent pill, the only moving element --></div>
  <button role="radio" aria-checked="true">Day</button>
  <button role="radio" aria-checked="false">Week</button>
  <button role="radio" aria-checked="false">Month</button>
  <button role="radio" aria-checked="false">Year</button>
</div>

Key points:

  • The pill is absolutely positioned and only ever animates transform: translateX (the demo writes it via a Motion motion value); slot buttons are normal document flow;
  • Two implementation routes: Motion layoutId (the same layoutId across slots, FLIP handled automatically — the BuildUI original) or a hand-rolled transform slot spring (semi-implicit Euler a = (−k(x−to) − cv)/m, velocity stored on dataset for interruption handoff, restDelta aligned with Motion’s source) (research 10 §2.1/§2.2); the demo uses Motion’s physics spring following the latter’s semantics;
  • ARIA is radiogroup/radio + aria-checked (single-selector semantics); arrow keys move, click selects.

Styling & Parameters

All three parameters are JS-type, assembled by the demo into the Motion spring and slots:

// Pill spring: stiffness 300 / damping 25 (mass 1) = the "medium-microbounce" tier, research 10 §2.3
animate(x, slotIndex * slotWidth, { type: "spring", stiffness: 300, damping: 25, mass: 1 });

// Readouts (computed locally, mass = 1): ω₀ = √k, ζ = c / (2√k)
const w0 = Math.sqrt(stiffness);            // 300 → 17.3 rad/s
const zeta = damping / (2 * w0);            // 25 → 0.722
const overshoot = zeta < 1
  ? Math.exp(-Math.PI * zeta / Math.sqrt(1 - zeta * zeta)) * 100  // 3.8%
  : 0;
const envelope5 = Math.log(20) / (zeta * w0) * 1000;             // 240ms

Where the numbers come from (research 10 §2.1/§2.3/§2.4):

  • BuildUI AnimatedTabs official recipe: bounce 0.2 / duration 0.6 (duration-based spring), converting to ζ≈0.8, overshoot≈1.5% — “bouncy but quiet”;
  • Recommended tiers (locally computed in Python, research 10 §2.3): fast-stable 700/40 (ζ0.756, overshoot 2.7%, t_peak 181ms), medium-microbounce 300/25 (ζ0.722, 3.8%, 262ms), slow-bouncy 200/15 (ζ0.530, 14.0%, 262ms), Motion physics default 100/10 (ζ0.500, 16.3%, 362ms);
  • Counter-example: stiffness:500, damping:50 is overdamped (ζ≈1.118) — zero overshoot, duller than the micro-bounce tier; compute ζ before copying;
  • The iOS system segmented indicator slides on a spring, but Apple has not published its stiffness/damping — unverified; the “medium-microbounce” tier is the standard stand-in.

Motion & Interaction

  • Slot-to-slot spring: clicking a slot sends the pill there on type:"spring"; rapid re-targets continue from the current position + current velocity (Motion physics-spring semantics) — never a from-rest restart (research 10 §7.3);
  • Live parameters: dragging stiffness/damping re-runs the current slot immediately; the readout converts ζ / overshoot / envelope→5% / t_peak in real time, building a number↔feel intuition;
  • Mirroring the iOS segmented control: equal-width segments, single selector (no action triggers mixed in), ≤5 on iPhone — the demo caps at 5 (HIG qualitative guidance, research 10 §2.4);
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move the selection on the radiogroup; aria-checked stays in sync;
  • Reduced motion: the spring is skipped and the pill lands on target directly; selection still rides aria-checked + text contrast;
  • Performance: only transform animates (pill travel) — compositor-friendly (research 10 §7.1).

Real-world examples

  • BuildUI AnimatedTabs: the source of the paradigm — layoutId + bounce 0.2/duration 0.6, with the radius inlined to get Motion’s scale correction (research 10 §2.1);
  • iOS system segmented controls (Calendar/Clocks etc.): the springing indicator is widely recognized system behavior, with equal widths and ≤5 segments per HIG; Apple has not published the animation parameters — unverified (research 10 §2.4);
  • shadcn/ui Tabs: same family of thinking — the ecosystem widely implements active indicators as “one slider + transform”; shadcn’s official buttons/overlays carry no press scaling, and its overlay numbers live in the overlay-entrance entry (research 10 §1.1/§3.1).

Parameter Sensitivity

ParameterTurning it up…Turning it down…
stiffnessω₀↑: fiercer launch, faster arrival; at the same ζ the waves pack tighter with the overshoot ratio unchangedmore lethargic; below 100 a long slot hop feels like drifting
dampingζ↑ toward critical: overshoot decays exponentially, “steady” feel; past ζ≥1 it turns overdamped — fast start, slow finishζ↓ multiplies rebound; ≤0.5 oscillates visibly, below 0.3 bounces like a ball
segmentsmore segments = narrower slots, shorter travel, choppier rhythmfewer segments = longer travel, a more “whole” feel; 5 is the iPhone ceiling (HIG)
Readouts (read-only)overshoot % and envelope time give a numeric mirror of the feel while draggingmirrored the other way; reference: ζ0.7 → 4.6% (bouncy-but-quiet), ζ0.8 → 1.5% (restrained), ζ1 → 0 (critical)

Working ranges: segmented default 300/25 (ζ≈0.72, overshoot <5%); 700/40 for “fast-stable”; 200/15 for the “bamboo-slip feel”; always compute ζ first — 0.7 ± 0.1 is the sweet spot for most interactions (research 10 §2.3, craft #3).

When to use 4
  • Segmented controls / tab indicators: one bubble sliding across slots; start at 300/25, drop toward 700/40 (fast-stable tier) for restraint
  • When the 'bamboo-slip feel' is the point: 200/15 (slow-bouncy tier, 14% overshoot) suits emphatic infrequent actions, not high-frequency switching
  • Mirroring the iOS segmented control: equal-width segments + single-selector semantics + a spring indicator; ≤5 segments on iPhone (HIG)
  • Interruptible sliding: clicking a new slot continues from the current position + velocity — never a from-rest restart
When not to use 4
  • More than 5 segments on iPhone or mixing in 'action trigger' segments — violates the HIG segmented-control guidance (research 10 §2.4)
  • Copying the folk 500/50 without computing ζ — an overdamped fake spring (ζ≈1.118), duller than the micro-bounce tier (research 10 §2.3)
  • Slot springs on progress bars / continuous quantities — the indicator means discrete slots; continuous values belong on progress bars (same caveat as the motion-spring entry)
  • Rendering a selected background per slot and animating each — keep exactly one persistent bubble (single-element travel), or slots will morph and jump
Accessibility

A segmented control is a single selector: the demo uses role=radiogroup + role=radio/aria-checked, arrow keys move between segments, Space selects (WAI-APG Radio Group semantics); the sliding indicator is visual enhancement only — selection is also carried by aria-checked and text contrast; under prefers-reduced-motion the indicator lands directly; motion is never the sole carrier of the selection change (HIG Motion: cancellable).

Browser support

The demo rides Motion (motion/react) and needs JS; without it the indicator stays in its initial slot. `layoutId` is a Motion layout-animation capability (works in all browsers — JS measurement + transform underneath). The spring readouts (ζ/overshoot/envelope) are computed locally from formulas, browser-independent.

Fallback

Under prefers-reduced-motion the spring is skipped and the indicator lands on the target slot directly; the selection must never be expressed by motion alone — the selected slot always carries a readable visual state (fill/text color); readouts keep showing the converted values.

Sources 5
