Aesthetic-Usability Effect Users judge beautiful designs as more usable: aesthetics correlates with *perceived* ease of use more strongly than with *actual* ease of use. Beauty buys tolerance — and can mask real problems. Community ★★★ WAI-ARIA APG Patterns The W3C ARIA Authoring Practices provides 30 component patterns; each ships keyboard interaction plus ARIA roles/states/properties in three sections — the first reference for building custom components. Web Standard ★★ Contrast Ratio Formula The full WCAG 2.1 derivation: 8-bit→[0,1]→γ decode→0.2126R+0.7152G+0.0722B relative luminance→(L1+0.05)/(L2+0.05) — feed two colors, get the ratio live, judged against the AA/AAA gates. Web Standard ★★★ Fitts's Law Time to acquire a target is a function of its distance and size: smaller and farther targets take longer and fail more. Make touch targets big, well spaced, and close to the focus area. Community ★★★ Law of Proximity Elements near each other are perceived as a group: spacing itself is grouping grammar. Tight inside, loose outside — relationships hold without a single line. Community ★★★ Goal-Gradient Effect Effort toward a goal intensifies as the goal gets closer. Give clear progress indicators, pre-seed 'artificial progress' at the start, and invest in the final stretch — the nearer the goal, the stronger each step's pull. Community ★★★ Doherty Threshold Productivity soars when human-computer interaction stays under 400ms: give feedback within 400ms, or kill the feeling of waiting with perceived performance (optimistic UI, skeletons). Community ★★★ Hick's Law Decision time grows with the number and complexity of choices: more alternatives, slower decisions. Cut options where response time matters, split complex tasks, and surface a recommended option. Community ★★★ Jakob's Law Users spend most of their time on other products and arrive with the expectations built there. Lean on familiar mental models; don't discard every familiar cue at redesign. Community ★★ Law of Similarity & Closure Visually similar elements (color/shape/size/orientation) group together; incomplete outlines are completed into whole figures. Similar look implies similar function; closure keeps broken shapes readable. Community ★★★ Keyboard Navigation Tab/Shift+Tab moves between components, arrow keys move inside composites (roving focus); focus indicators stay visible, focus is never lost, and modal traps must release via Esc and return focus. Web Standard ★★★ Miller's Law The average person keeps about 7±2 items in working memory. The fix is chunking — but the 'magical number seven' must not justify unnecessary design limitations. Community ★★ Pareto Principle Roughly 80% of effects come from roughly 20% of causes — and 'roughly' is part of the law. Identify the vital few areas that yield the most and concentrate effort there; never apply it mechanically. Community ★★★ Serial Position Effect First and last items in a series are best remembered (primacy + recency): put the least important in the middle and key actions at the far left and right of navigation. Community ★★ prefers-contrast The CSS media query detecting a requested contrast preference: no-preference / more / less / custom; focus rings, dividers and disabled text should all respond. Web Standard ★★★ Peak-End Rule People judge an experience by its peak moment and its ending, not the average. Design the highest-intensity and closing moments; eliminate negative peaks deliberately. Community ★★★ Von Restorff Effect Among similar objects, the one that differs is the one remembered. Make key actions visually distinctive — with restraint, and never by color alone. Community ★★★ Tesler's Law Every system carries irreducible complexity — borne either by the system or by the user. The goal of design is moving that inherent complexity off the user and into the system. Community ★★ WCAG Quick Checklist A WCAG 2.2 quick checklist organized by the four POUR principles (Perceivable/Operable/Understandable/Robust): audit item by item at your conformance level (A/AA/AAA) before shipping.
