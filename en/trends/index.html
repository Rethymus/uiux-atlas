Design trend tracker

10 trends

Ten directional signals distilled from 2023–2026 annual trend reports and first-party generation stats: which styles are rising, which are cooling. Every trend carries its stat basis and research source, cross-linked to evergreen entries on this site.

Trends are point-in-time snapshots (research dated 2026-11-20), not live data; the refresh recipe and stat definitions live in src/data/trends.ts.

Rising ↑ 5

↑ Rising

Glass materials keep climbing

Superdesign generation share climbed from 7.5% (Jan 2026) to 9.9% (May, 7,866 projects) — the second most requested style; Apple's Liquid Glass also fuels real "liquid glass vs glassmorphism" search demand.

Basis Request share within one AI design tool (superdesign.dev, 208k generations), not market adoption.

Source out/research/03-visual-style-trends.md §2.2「兴起」第 1 条 · superdesign.dev/styles/glassmorphism

↑ Rising

Anti-AI crafting fights the hyper-slick

Hand-drawn, scrapbook, collage and visible craft marks are rising; Creative Bloq names Anti-AI Crafting the defining 2026 trend — "human-driven craft as the antidote to AI's hyper-slick visual language" (Landor global ECD).

Basis Trend reporting and expert interviews (Creative Bloq / Landor), no share statistics.

Source out/research/03-visual-style-trends.md §2.2「兴起」第 3 条 · creativebloq.com 2026 图形设计趋势

↑ Rising

A new skeuomorphic material family

Skeuomorphism returns as new branches — manuscript, wood, glowing biophilic and Y2K tech — spilling into chrome/liquid metal and Spatial UI (visionOS language leaking into 2D product screens).

Basis Year-report consensus (daisyUI 2026), not a share statistic; what returns is a new material family — distinct from the cooling of soft-UI Neumorphism.

Source out/research/03-visual-style-trends.md §2.2「兴起」第 4 条 · trends.daisyui.com/ui-design-trends-2026/

Claymorphism nudges up in its niche

Generation share edged up from 0.03% (Jan 2026) to 0.08% (May), settling into children's products, edtech and illustrated landing pages — a 2022 hotspot matured into a niche.

Basis Request share within one AI design tool (superdesign.dev, 208k generations), not market adoption; tiny absolute values, directional only.

↓ Cooling

Stylistic brutalism is past its peak

After peaking at 3.6% in Mar 2026 it fell back (3.3% in May, 2.37% in June); functional brutalism (Craigslist/HN) stays evergreen, while deliberately styled brutalism dates fast.

Basis Request share within one AI design tool (superdesign.dev), not market adoption; this site indexes the commercial branch Neo-Brutalism, not stylistic brutalism itself.

Source out/research/03-visual-style-trends.md §2.2「退潮」第 1 条 · superdesign.dev/styles/brutalism

↓ Cooling

Neumorphism fades toward zero

Share fell 0.69%→0.41%, about 1/20 of glass; typical edge contrast is ~1.4:1 and cannot meet WCAG's 3:1 while keeping the soft look — accessibility is the hard blocker, not taste.

Basis Request share within one AI design tool (superdesign.dev), not market adoption.

↓ Cooling

Bento grids mature into convention

Hovering at 1–1.6% all year (1.6% in Jan easing to 1.3% in May), bento is no longer a novelty signal — it cooled from fashion into a standard layout convention; cell-size-as-priority still holds.

Basis Request share within one AI design tool (superdesign.dev), not market adoption; the slow decline is maturation (enlighten: "Maturing Pattern"), not exit.

Source out/research/03-visual-style-trends.md §2.2「退潮」第 3 条 · superdesign.dev/styles/bento-grid;design-styles.enlighten-media.net/pages/bento-ui/

↓ Cooling

Full-screen Liquid Glass gets pushed back

NN/g rules "don't copy Apple's mistake" (worse readability, smaller tap targets); Apple HIG keeps it off the content layer, and iOS 27 walks back default transparency — use glass, but confine it to nav and controls.

Basis Authoritative review and official-spec moves (NN/g / Apple), not a share statistic; not contradicted by rising glass — what rises is restrained usage.

Source out/research/03-visual-style-trends.md §2.2「退潮」第 4 条 · nngroup.com/articles/liquid-glass;en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid_Glass

↓ Cooling

Pure minimalism yields to bold minimalism

Minimalism with strong contrast and confident typography is the new direction (Digital Synopsis 2026, item 13); minimalism itself remains the baseline — 9–12% of superdesign requests all year, #1. What cools is characterless minimalism, not minimalism.

Basis Request share within one AI design tool (superdesign.dev, 208k generations), not market adoption; the "yielding" verdict comes from Digital Synopsis 2026 — two different bases, shown side by side.

Source out/research/03-visual-style-trends.md §2.2「退潮」第 5 条 · superdesign.dev/styles/minimalist-web-design;digitalsynopsis.com 2026 趋势
