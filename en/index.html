AI Answer States Three non-normal answer states: the refusal card (display, don't parse), generic error text + retry, and structured citations (source cards/badges/footnotes) — nothing leaks on failure, every citation traces to a passage. Community ★★★ Generative UI Model output → host component mapping: seven-state tool badges, schema-driven trees, progressive mid-stream rendering — the model can only request UI; rendering power always stays with the allowlist. Community ★★★ AI Suggestion Chips Guidance for what comes next after an answer: click-to-send as the official usage, turn-boundary-only refresh discipline, post-click states against double firing — suggestions are signposts, not noise. Community ★★★ AI Thinking Indicator Form selection for AI reasoning waits: NN/g's three response-time limits pick the form, shimmer thinking labels, dot pulses and step cards with auto-collapse — the wait is acknowledged, not decorated. Community ★★★ AI Streaming Response Progressive rendering of AI answers: self-healing unclosed markdown, a trailing caret, sticky-bottom scrolling with user-interrupt, and two-level throttling — the layout stays stable wherever the token stream is. Community ★★★ AI Streaming Table Tables that grow with the token stream: row-level results of block memoization, fixed skeletons locking column widths, in-table scrolling that never breaks the session lock — wide tables stream too, without layout jumps.
