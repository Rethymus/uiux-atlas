Learning paths

6 paths

The paths layer: ordered learning routes through existing entries and recipes — each path states who it is for, what you can do when you finish, which entries/recipes it cites in order (each step with a "why now" note), and closes with a self-check list (progress is saved in your browser). Data-driven from the registry (src/data/paths.ts), filled in batch by batch.

Zero to Glass Early-career frontend developers who want to turn glassmorphism from a screenshot into a production-grade component they can write, tune and replicate — not just a blur() filter. 6 steps Open path → →
Motion Fundamentals Frontend devs who ship pages but wing the motion: build a parameterized view of duration × easing × springs instead of pasting curve libraries. 5 steps Open path → →
Color Systems Devs and designers moving from gut-feel color picks to a computable color system: OKLCH, semantic tokens and accessible contrast in one line. 5 steps Open path → →
Design Tokens Frontend/design engineers turning a design language into code assets: three-layer tokens, multi-theme mapping and registry engineering. 6 steps Open path → →
AI Interface Patterns Interface engineers building AI products: the full pattern set from streaming responses and thinking states to suggestion chips and generative UI. 7 steps Open path → →
Accessibility Essentials Teams turning accessibility from a pre-launch checklist into daily craft: WCAG checklist, keyboard navigation and the contrast ratio formula, item by item. 5 steps Open path → →
