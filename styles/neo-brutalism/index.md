## 适用场景 / When to use

- SaaS 落地页与独立产品品牌站——2023 年起的「新酷」主场(调研 03§6) / SaaS landing pages and indie product sites — the style's 'new cool' home since 2023 (research 03 §6)
- 设计社区、作品集、活动与营销页,需要强个性与反抛光姿态 / Design communities, portfolios, campaign pages — strong personality and an anti-polish stance
- 表单与卡片仍走常规 UX——新粗野主义「把常规 UX 包在响亮块面里」,不牺牲可用性 / Forms and cards keep conventional UX — neo-brutalism 'wraps conventional UX in loud blocks' without sacrificing usability

## 何时不用 / When not to use

- 长文阅读界面——粗描边与高饱和色块对持续阅读是持续噪音 / Long-form reading — fat outlines and saturated blocks are persistent noise
- 企业级严肃产品主界面与医疗/金融后台——风格气质与信任基调冲突 / Serious enterprise shells and medical/finance backends — the look clashes with trust cues
- 多强调色堆叠——判据是单一饱和强调色,糖果色互相冲撞会失去印刷感 / Stacking multiple accents — the recipe is a single saturated accent; clashing candies lose the print feel
