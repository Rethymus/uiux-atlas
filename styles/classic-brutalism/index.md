## 适用场景 / When to use

- 功能型内容站:文档、论坛、目录、新闻聚合——Craigslist/Hacker News 一脉的「常青用法」(调研:功能型粗野主义常青) / Functional content sites: docs, forums, directories, aggregators — the evergreen Craigslist/Hacker News lineage (research: functional brutalism endures)
- 宣言式作品集与杂志专题——用「反装饰」本身做表达,2014 画廊一脉的风格型用法 / Manifesto portfolios and magazine features — anti-decoration as the message, the style-driven 2014 gallery lineage
- 原型与 MVP——零样式预算下用浏览器默认渲染先上线 / Prototypes and MVPs — ship first on the browser's default rendering with a zero style budget
- 性能极限场景:弱网、低功耗设备——无字体/无装饰图片就是最快的首屏 / Performance-critical contexts: slow networks, low-power devices — no fonts and no decorative images is the fastest first paint there is

## 何时不用 / When not to use

- 消费级品牌站——「未抛光」与品牌信任感直接冲突 / Consumer brand sites — 'unpolished' clashes directly with brand trust
- 数据可视化与富交互应用——黑白灰单色撑不起多维信息编码 / Data visualization and rich apps — a black/white/gray palette cannot carry multi-dimensional encoding
- 把「风格型粗野」当长期皮肤:调研明示其易过时(2026-03 需求峰值 3.6% 后回落) / Treating style-driven brutalism as a long-term skin: research shows it dates fast (demand peaked at 3.6% in March 2026, then fell)
- 密集表单与支付流——默认渲染的控件外观在品牌一致性上会失守 / Dense forms and checkout flows — default-styled controls leak brand consistency
