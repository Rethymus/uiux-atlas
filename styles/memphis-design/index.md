## 适用场景 / When to use

- 品牌识别、活动海报与社媒图——调研 03§13:网页上多为品牌资产/图案/活动页而非整站语言 / Brand identity, event posters and social graphics — research 03 §13: on the web it's mostly brand assets/patterns/campaign pages, not whole-site language
- 面向年轻受众的插画型落地页局部(页眉横幅、章节分隔、贴纸挂件) / Illustrated landing-page sections for young audiences (hero banners, section dividers, sticker badges)
- Gen-Z 80s 复古潮下的限量联名与快闪活动——调研 03§13 记载其全面复兴正在发生 / Limited collabs and pop-ups riding the Gen-Z 80s revival — research 03 §13 records the revival as ongoing

## 何时不用 / When not to use

- 正文排版与数据表格——图案堆叠直接吃掉文字层级,冲突色持续干扰扫读 / Body typography and data tables — pattern stacking buries text hierarchy and clashing hues fight scanning
- 无障碍敏感场景(医疗、政务、银行)——「无调和」的配色纪律与 3:1/4.5:1 对比度要求天然冲突 / Accessibility-sensitive contexts (health, government, banking) — the no-harmony palette discipline collides with 3:1/4.5:1 contrast rules
- 整站全屏铺满——enlighten 明示孟菲斯在网页上是局部语言,强行整站会迅速视觉疲劳 / Whole-site saturation — enlighten is explicit that Memphis is a local language on the web; forcing it site-wide fatigues fast
