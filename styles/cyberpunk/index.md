## 适用场景 / When to use

- 娱乐与游戏品牌站——Cyberpunk 2077 官网(扫描线+霓虹+全屏视频)是标杆案例(调研 03§14) / Entertainment and game brand sites — the Cyberpunk 2077 site (scanlines + neon + full-screen video) is the benchmark (research 03 §14)
- 音乐、夜生活、电竞活动页与限定发布皮肤 / Music, nightlife and esports campaign pages and limited-release skins
- 短时停留的沉浸式首屏——强氛围、低信息密度 / Short-stay immersive heroes — strong atmosphere, low information density

## 何时不用 / When not to use

- 需要长时间阅读的界面——低对比霓虹文字伤害可读性(调研明示局限) / Interfaces for sustained reading — low-contrast neon hurts legibility (a limitation the research names)
- 企业产品与生产力工具——受众窄、时代感强易过时 / Enterprise products and productivity tools — narrow audience and a dated feel over time
- 重资产预算紧张的项目——该风格常伴随视频/粒子/着色器成本(调研明示) / Projects without heavy-asset budgets — the style usually drags video/particle/shader costs (named in the research)
