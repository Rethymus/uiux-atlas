## 适用场景 / When to use

- 健康/自然/环保主题的产品页——水、天空、草地的语义与主题天然契合 / Health/nature/eco product pages — water, sky and grass semantics fit the subject natively
- 面向大众的轻量工具与启动器(天气、日历、播放器)——气泡按钮大而圆润、光泽提供「可按压」暗示 / Mass-market lightweight tools and launchers (weather, calendar, player) — big round bubble buttons whose gloss hints pressability
- 怀旧营销与「千禧一代童年」主题活动——Aero 是 2005–2013 世代最具辨识度的屏幕记忆 / Nostalgia marketing and 'millennial childhood' campaigns — Aero is the most recognizable screen memory of the 2005–2013 generation

## 何时不用 / When not to use

- 高密度信息面板——光泽高光与气泡形会持续消耗注意力,表格/仪表盘不成立 / Dense information panels — gloss and bubble shapes keep draining attention; tables/dashboards can't carry it
- 专业开发/终端工具——「自然+光泽」的语义与工程师语境错位,可信度受损 / Developer/terminal tools — the 'nature + gloss' semantics misalign with engineer contexts and hurt credibility
- 低对比警示信息——白色椭圆高光下的浅色文字对比度极易不足,警示场景需换实底 / Low-contrast warning content — pale text over white elliptical gloss fails contrast easily; warnings need solid fields
