## 适用场景 / When to use

- 音乐/时尚潮流的活动发布页、专辑与演出物料(调研 03§12:当下由 Charli XCX、aespa/XG 等音乐文化推动) / Music/fashion campaign pages, album and gig collateral (research 03 §12: currently driven by Charli XCX, aespa/XG and peers)
- 千禧主题的营销彩蛋与限时活动页——短时、情绪型场景最能吃下强烈年代感 / Millennium-themed marketing easter eggs and limited-time pages — short, mood-driven scenes absorb the strong period flavor best
- 低信息密度装饰层:徽章、挂件、加载彩蛋,配 HUD 读数与像素纹理点题 / Low-density decorative layers: badges, widgets, loading easter eggs, with HUD readouts and pixel textures to set the tone

## 何时不用 / When not to use

- 长文阅读与数据密集界面——铬渐变与荧光色持续抢占注意力,信息层级被装饰淹没 / Long-form reading and data-dense screens — chrome gradients and fluorescent hues keep stealing attention and bury hierarchy
- 把果冻半透明色用于关键操作主色——alpha 稀释对比度,表单与警示场景不成立 / Jelly translucency as the primary action color — alpha dilutes contrast; forms and warning contexts can't carry it
- 需要多年复用的品牌系统——年代感是双刃剑,调研记载其复兴与经济情绪周期相关,过时风险高 / Brand systems meant to last years — period flavor cuts both ways; the research ties its revival to economic moods, so obsolescence risk is high
